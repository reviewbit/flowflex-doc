# Broadcasts overview

A broadcast sends an approved template to a list or segment of contacts. Each customer gets it as a personal message in their own chat, with their name and details filled in, and any reply comes straight to your team in **Messaging**. Afterwards, the broadcast's report shows how many people received it, read it, tapped a link, and replied, and lets you send a follow-up to any of those groups.

Rangoli Store, a saree shop in Kochi, uses broadcasts for its Diwali sale. Two weeks before the festival, it sends repeat buyers an early-access offer with a **Shop now** button. By the next morning, the team knows exactly who read it and who tapped through, and sends a reminder only to them.

Businesses use broadcasts for festive sales and new arrivals, back-in-stock alerts, appointment and fee reminders, admission dates, event invitations, and loyalty rewards.

## How a broadcast works

1. **Connect your WhatsApp number** in **Channels**. You do this once. See [Connect a WhatsApp channel](whatsapp/connect-channel.md).
2. **Create a template and get it approved.** Every broadcast uses a template that Meta has approved, such as `diwali_sale_2026_en`. See [Create a template](whatsapp/create-template.md).
3. **Choose the audience.** Pick a list such as "Repeat buyers – Oct 2026", or a [segment](audience/segments.md) that updates itself. You can also [import](audience/import-export.md) new contacts first.
4. **Schedule and publish.** Set the date, time and daily send count, send a test to your own phone, then publish. See [Send a broadcast](broadcasts/send-broadcast.md).
5. **Read the results and follow up.** Open the broadcast from **Broadcasts** to see who received, read, replied and tapped a link, and why any messages failed. Send a follow-up to the people who engaged. See [Results and errors](broadcasts/results-and-errors.md).

> [!NOTE]
> You can only broadcast **Approved** templates from a **Connected** channel. Contacts who opted out of WhatsApp marketing are left out of Marketing broadcasts automatically.
