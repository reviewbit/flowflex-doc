# Pre-send checklist and FAQ

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] The channel is **Connected**, its Health is **Available**, and its quality rating is High or Medium.
- [ ] The template is **Approved** and in the right category. Marketing templates have the opt-out button.
- [ ] Every contact opted in, and no opted-out contacts were re-added.
- [ ] Every Audience-field variable has a fallback.
- [ ] Media and links work, and links use your own domain.
- [ ] Time zone, start time, and end date are right, and the audience fits within daily count × days.
- [ ] **Retry failed messages after 48 hrs** is on for large marketing sends.
- [ ] The wallet balance covers the whole audience.
- [ ] A test message was received and checked on a real phone.

## FAQ

**Why can't I see my template in Select Template?**
It is not Approved yet, or it belongs to another channel's WABA.

**Can I edit an approved template?**
Create a new version (for example `_v2`) and submit it for review. Don't change the wording while a broadcast is running.

**Why did fewer people receive it than I selected?**
Opted-out contacts are excluded and invalid or duplicate numbers are skipped. Meta also holds some messages back (131049, 130472), and batches after the end date are never sent.

**Can I send immediately?**
Set the schedule a few minutes ahead. The start time cannot be in the past.

**How do I raise my daily limit?**
Complete Meta Business Verification, keep quality High, and keep reaching more unique recipients. See [Grow the Portfolio limit](broadcasts/connect-channel.md#grow-the-portfolio-limit).

**Do replies come into FlowFlex?**
Yes, in **Messaging → Conversations**. Within 24 hours of a reply, your team can answer with free-form messages.
