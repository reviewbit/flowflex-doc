# Pre-send checklist and FAQ

Most broadcast problems come from a few things: an unhealthy channel, a missing fallback, a broken link, or a schedule too short for the audience. A few minutes with this list catches them before your customers do.

Before Rangoli Store publishes "Diwali sale – repeat buyers – Oct 2026", Alex Lee runs through the list below and sends a test to Alex's own phone. The test shows a broken **Shop now** link, which Alex fixes before anyone else sees it.

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] The channel is **Connected**, its Health is **Available**, and its quality rating is High or Medium.
- [ ] The template is **Approved** and in the right category. Marketing templates have the opt-out button.
- [ ] Every contact opted in, and no opted-out contacts were added back.
- [ ] Every **Audience field** variable has a fallback.
- [ ] Media and links work, and links use your own domain.
- [ ] **Time zone**, start time and **End date** are right, and the audience fits within the daily send count × the number of days.
- [ ] **Retry failed messages after 48 hrs** is on for large marketing sends.
- [ ] The wallet balance covers the whole audience.
- [ ] A test message arrived on a real phone and you checked it.

## FAQ

### Why can't I see my template in Select Template?

It isn't Approved yet, or it belongs to a different channel's WhatsApp Business Account.

### Can I edit an approved template?

Create a new version, such as `diwali_sale_2026_en_v2`, and submit it for review. Don't change the wording while a broadcast is running.

### Why did fewer people receive it than I selected?

Opted-out contacts are left out, and invalid or duplicate numbers are skipped. Meta also holds some messages back (131049, 130472), and batches scheduled after the end date are never sent. See [Common errors](results-and-errors.md#common-errors).

### Can I send immediately?

Set the start time a few minutes ahead. It can't be in the past.

### How do I raise my daily limit?

Complete Meta Business Verification, keep quality High, and keep reaching more unique recipients. See [Grow the Portfolio limit](../whatsapp/connect-channel.md#grow-the-portfolio-limit).

### Do replies come into FlowFlex?

Yes, in **Messaging** → **Conversations**. For 24 hours after a customer replies, your team can answer with free-form messages.
