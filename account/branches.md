# Branches

A branch keeps its own WhatsApp channels, audience and work apart from the other branches. Rangoli Store runs a **Bengaluru** branch and a **Kochi** branch. The Kochi team sends offers to Kochi customers from the Kochi number, and never sees Bengaluru's chats or contacts.

Each branch has its own:

* WhatsApp channels, and the templates of each channel's WhatsApp Business Account
* broadcasts
* flows and workers
* audience: contacts, lists, segments, custom fields, imports and exports
* tags
* conversations and custom chat folders
* integrations

Users see a branch only if their access includes it. See [Teams](teams.md).

## Manage branches

Go to **Settings** → **Branches**. Only Admins see this page in the sidebar.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/branches.png" alt="Branches page with the active Kochi and Bengaluru branches and their locations"><figcaption><p>Branches page</p></figcaption></figure>

The **Branches** page shows how many branches you have and lists each one with its **Branch** (logo and name), **Location** (the address from **Branch location**) and **Status**, which is **Active** or **Disabled by plan**. Click the edit icon in the **Action** column to change a branch, and use **Search branches** to find one.

## Create a branch

When Rangoli Store opened its Kochi store, Alex Lee added a Kochi branch so the new team could work on its own.

1. Click **Create branch**. FlowFlex first checks that your plan has room for another branch, then opens **Create new branch**.
2. Optional: upload a logo. JPG or PNG, under 5 MB.
3. Enter a **Branch name**, such as `Kochi`. It needs at least 3 characters; otherwise you see **Branch name must be at least 3 characters long**.
4. Optional: start typing an address in **Branch location** and pick a suggestion.
5. Click **Create branch**. You see **Branch created successfully!**

## Edit a branch

1. Click the edit icon on the branch's row. **Update branch** opens.
2. Change the logo, **Branch name** or **Branch location**.
3. Click **Update branch**. If nothing changed, you see **No changes detected**.

## Branch limits

Your plan sets how many branches you can have. These messages tell you when you've reached the limit.

| Message                                                                               | Cause                                                                       | Fix                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Branch limit reached. You can purchase additional branches for … each in Billing.** | You use all the branches in your plan and add-ons                           | Add **Additional Branches** on [Plans](plans-and-add-ons.md). FlowFlex opens **Plans** for you. |
| **Branch limit reached. Please upgrade your plan to add more branches.**              | Your plan has no branch add-on                                              | Upgrade on [Plans](plans-and-add-ons.md)                                                        |
| **Disabled by plan**                                                                  | You have more branches than your plan allows, for example after a downgrade | Hover over the status to see why. Add branches back to your plan to re-enable it.               |

You can't edit or switch to a branch that is **Disabled by plan**. Its edit icon is unavailable, and in **Switch branch** it's greyed out with the reason.

## Switch branch

1. Click your name at the top right. The card shows your organisation and the branch you're in, such as **Kochi branch**.
2. Click **Switch**. The **Switch branch** panel opens.
3. Click a branch. The branch you're in is listed first, marked **Current**. With more than six branches, use **Search branches** to find one.

Click **Manage** in **Switch branch** to open the **Branches** page.

> \[!TIP] The branch name under your name shows where you are. Check it before you create a template or send a broadcast.
