# Branches

Split your organisation into separate workspaces, such as one per store or city, and switch between them.

A branch keeps its own WhatsApp channels, audience and work apart from the other branches. Rangoli Store runs a **Bengaluru** branch and a **Kochi** branch. The Kochi team sends offers to Kochi customers from the Kochi number, and never sees Bengaluru's chats or contacts.

Each branch has its own:

- WhatsApp channels, and the templates of each channel's WhatsApp Business Account
- broadcasts
- flows and campaigns
- audience: contacts, lists, segments, custom fields, imports and exports
- tags
- conversations and custom chat folders
- integrations

Users see a branch only if their access includes it. See [Teams](account/teams.md).

## Manage branches

Go to **Settings** → **Branches**. Only Admins see this page in the sidebar.

<img src="assets/account/branches.png" alt="Settings, Branches">

The table lists each branch with its logo, **Name**, **Location** (the address from **Branch location**) and **Status**, which is **Active** or **Disabled by Plan**. Click the edit icon in the **Action** column to change a branch, and use **Search by name** to find one.

## Create a branch

When Rangoli Store opened its Kochi store, Alex Lee added a Kochi branch so the new team could work on its own.

1. Click **Create branch**. FlowFlex first checks that your plan has room for another branch.
2. Optional: upload a logo. JPG or PNG, under 5 MB.
3. Enter a **Branch name**, such as `Kochi`. It needs at least 3 characters.
4. Optional: start typing an address in **Branch location** and pick a suggestion.
5. Click **Create branch**. You see **Branch created successfully!**

## Edit a branch

1. Click the edit icon on the branch's row.
2. Change the logo, **Branch name** or **Branch location**.
3. Click **Update branch**. If nothing changed, you see **No changes detected**.

## Branch limits

Your plan sets how many branches you can have. These messages tell you when you've reached the limit.

| Message | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Branch limit reached. You can purchase additional branches for … each in Billing.** | You use all the branches in your plan and add-ons | Add **Additional Branches** on [Plans](account/plans-and-add-ons.md). FlowFlex opens **Plans** for you. |
| **Branch limit reached. Please upgrade your plan to add more branches.** | Your plan has no branch add-on | Upgrade on [Plans](account/plans-and-add-ons.md) |
| **Disabled by Plan** | You have more branches than your plan allows, for example after a downgrade | Hover over the status to see why. Add branches back to your plan to re-enable it. |

You can't edit or switch to a branch that is **Disabled by Plan**. Trying shows **Cannot edit branch.** with the reason.

## Switch branch

1. Click your name and branch at the top right.
2. Choose **Switch branch**.
3. In **My Branches**, click a branch. The branch you're in is marked **Active**.

Click **Manage** in **My Branches** to open **Settings** → **Branches**.

> [!TIP]
> The branch name under your name shows where you are. Check it before you create a template or send a broadcast.
