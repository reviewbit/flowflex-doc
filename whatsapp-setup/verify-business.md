# Verify your business on Meta

Verify your business in Meta Business Suite so your WhatsApp number can reach more customers and unlock features that need a verified business.

Business verification is Meta's check that your Business portfolio belongs to a real, registered business. You do it in Meta Business Suite, not in FlowFlex. FlowFlex shows the result on your channel page as **Business Verification Status**.

You can connect a number and start sending before you verify. Until you do, your Business portfolio can message up to 250 unique customers in a rolling 24 hours.

Rangoli Store has connected its Kochi counter number to FlowFlex, but its Diwali sale broadcast stops at 250 customers a day. Alex Lee, the Admin, verifies the business in Meta Business Suite with the store's GST certificate and an electricity bill. A few days later the limit moves to 2,000, and the broadcast reaches the full list.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

* **Full control of the Business portfolio:** only someone with full control can start verification.
* **Legal business name and address:** exactly as they appear on your registration documents.
* **Business phone number, email or website:** Meta sends a code to one of these, or checks the website domain, to confirm the business is yours.
* **A document that shows your legal name:** for example a trade licence, certificate of incorporation, or tax registration such as a GST or VAT certificate.
* **A document that shows your address or phone number:** for example a utility or phone bill, or a bank statement in the business's name.

Documents must be current, clear and complete, and the name and address on them must match what you type in.

## Check whether you can verify

Go to [Security Center](https://business.facebook.com/settings/security) in Meta Business Suite and select your Business portfolio. You see one of these:

| You see                         | What it means                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Start verification**          | You can verify now. Follow the steps below.                                                                       |
| **Ineligible for verification** | Meta isn't offering verification to this portfolio yet. Keep sending good-quality messages and check again later. |
| **Verified**                    | You're done. Nothing more to do.                                                                                  |

If you can't open Security Center, you don't have full control of the Business portfolio. Ask its admin to give you full control, or to verify the business themselves.

## Verify the business

1. In [Security Center](https://business.facebook.com/settings/security), click **Start verification**.
2. Enter the legal business name, address, phone number and website. Use the details on your documents, not a shortened or trading name.
3. If Meta lists a matching business from public records, select it. If it doesn't, choose the option for a business that isn't listed and continue.
4. Upload a document that shows your legal business name. If Meta asks, upload a second document that shows your address or phone number.
5. Choose how to confirm the business is yours: email, phone call, text message, WhatsApp message, or domain verification. Enter the code Meta sends you. For domain verification, follow [Meta's domain steps](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/321167023127050).
6. Click **Done**. Meta reviews the application and emails the admins of the Business portfolio with the result.

Review usually takes a few working days. You can check progress in Security Center at any time.

## After you're verified

* Security Center shows **Verified**, and your channel page in FlowFlex shows the new **Business Verification Status**. It can take a little while for FlowFlex to show the change.
* Your portfolio limit moves from 250 to 2,000 unique customers in 24 hours. Meta raises it further as your quality stays high. See [Grow the portfolio limit](../whatsapp/connect-channel.md#grow-the-portfolio-limit).

If you later change the business name, address or phone number in Meta Business Suite, Meta asks you to verify again.

## Verification is not the blue tick

Three things get mixed up:

| Name                                  | What it is                                                                       | Where you get it                              |
| ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Business verification                 | Meta's check that your portfolio belongs to a real business. No badge.           | Security Center in Meta Business Suite        |
| Official Business Account (blue tick) | A badge next to your WhatsApp name for notable brands. Needs verification first. | Request it for the number in WhatsApp Manager |
| Meta Verified for business            | A paid monthly subscription with a badge and extra support.                      | Meta's subscription sign-up                   |

FlowFlex shows the first two on the channel page as **Business Verification Status** and **Official Business Account**.

## Common problems

| Problem                           | Cause                                                                      | Fix                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Application rejected              | The name or address doesn't match the documents                            | Enter the details exactly as they appear on the documents, then apply again. See [Meta's rejection help](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2342133782492969). |
| Document not accepted             | Blurry, cropped, expired, or in a name other than the business's           | Upload a clear, current scan of the full document.                                                                                                                 |
| No code arrives                   | The phone number or email in Meta Business Suite is wrong or not monitored | Choose another method, or use domain verification.                                                                                                                 |
| **Start verification** is missing | You don't have full control, or the portfolio is ineligible for now        | Ask the admin for full control, or see [Check whether you can verify](verify-business.md#check-whether-you-can-verify).                                            |
| FlowFlex still shows unverified   | The status hasn't reached FlowFlex yet                                     | Wait a few hours. If it still shows unverified after a day, contact support.                                                                                       |
