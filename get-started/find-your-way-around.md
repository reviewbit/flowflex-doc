# Find your way around

FlowFlex has a sidebar on the left, a top bar along the top, and the page you are working on in the middle. What you see in the sidebar depends on your role.

When Alex Lee signs in to Rangoli Store, the Home page shows the **Get started** checklist. Alex clicks **Invite members** to invite Chris Taylor, then uses **Switch** in the profile drawer to move from the **Kochi** branch to **Bengaluru** and check that branch's broadcasts.

## Sidebar

The sidebar has two groups, **MAIN** and **SETTINGS**. Click the FlowFlex logo at the top to go back to the Home page. Click the arrow on the sidebar's edge to collapse it to icons.

Under **MAIN**:

* **Workflows** (Beta): build automated WhatsApp flows and run them. The page has three tabs: **Flows**, **Workers** and **Saved data**. See [Flows overview](../flows/overview.md) and [Workers](../flows/workers.md). If you don't see **Workflows**, contact FlowFlex support.
* **Broadcasts**: send one message to many contacts. See [Broadcasts overview](../broadcasts/overview.md).
* **Templates**: create WhatsApp message templates. See [Create a template](../whatsapp/create-template.md).
* **Channels**: connect and manage WhatsApp numbers. See [Connect a WhatsApp channel](../whatsapp/connect-channel.md).
* **Audience**: your contacts, lists and segments. See [Audience](../audience/overview.md).
* **Messaging** → **Conversations**: chat with your contacts. A pill shows how many messages are unread. See [Conversations](../messaging/conversations.md).
* **Messaging** → **Settings**: messaging settings. See [Messaging settings](../messaging/settings.md).
* **Messaging** → your folders: any custom chat folders you created.

Under **SETTINGS**:

* **Integrations**: connect your own systems. See [Integrations](../integrations/overview.md).
* **Billing**: your billing overview and wallet. See [Billing and wallet](../account/billing-and-wallet.md).
* **Settings** → **Account**: your own profile.
* **Settings** → **Plans**: compare and choose plans. See [Plans and add-ons](../account/plans-and-add-ons.md).
* **Settings** → **Teams**: invite people and manage teams. See [Teams](../account/teams.md).
* **Settings** → **Branches**: add and manage branches. See [Branches](../account/branches.md).

> \[!NOTE] You only see the items your role allows. For example, **Broadcasts** needs **Broadcast view**. **Billing**, **Plans**, **Teams** and **Branches** need **Full control**. See [Roles and permissions](../account/roles-and-permissions.md).

If your plan is not active, clicking a sidebar item shows **Upgrade Required** with an **Upgrade** button.

## Home page

The Home page greets you by your first name, for example **Good to see you, Alex.**, with the line **Let's make a few good connections today.** To return to it from anywhere, click the FlowFlex logo in the sidebar.

Below the greeting, the banner **Small conversations. Lasting connections.** has a **Let's talk** button that opens Conversations.

### Get started checklist

The **Get started** checklist tracks your setup, for example **Get started (2/4)**. Click its header to collapse or expand it. Once every step is done, the checklist disappears.

Each step gets a tick when it's done. Only the step you're on shows its button:

1. **Set up your account:** **View account** opens your account settings.
2. **Connect your WhatsApp number:** **Connect number** starts the Meta signup. See [Connect a WhatsApp channel](../whatsapp/connect-channel.md).
3. **Connect your tools:** **Explore integrations** opens Integrations. See [Integrations](../integrations/overview.md).
4. **Invite your team:** **Invite members** opens the invite page. See [Teams](../account/teams.md).

For the full setup order, see [Set up your account](../getting-started.md).

### Explore Flowflex

The **Explore Flowflex** cards take you to the main areas. You see only the cards your role allows:

* **Messaging:** **Open conversations**.
* **Audience:** **Explore your audience**.
* **Flows:** **Open flows**.
* **Broadcasts:** **Open broadcasts**.
* **Need a hand?:** **Ask the AI Assistant** opens the [AI Assistant](../help/ai-assistant.md).
* **Watch a walkthrough:** **Watch videos** opens FlowFlex's video tutorials.

## Top bar

The top bar shows where you are, then your wallet balance, your name and the notifications bell on the right.

### Wallet balance

The chip with a coin icon shows your wallet balance. Hover over it to see the full balance and the time of the last activity. Click it to open your wallet. See [Billing and wallet](../account/billing-and-wallet.md).

Recent usage can take a little while to appear in your balance.

### Your name and branch

Your name shows above the current branch, for example **Alex** above **Kochi**. Click it to open the profile drawer.

The drawer starts with your name and email. Below them, a card shows your organisation and branch, such as **Rangoli Store** and **Kochi branch**. Click **Switch** on the card to change branch. See [Switch branch](find-your-way-around.md#switch-branch).

Under **Account**:

* **Account settings:** opens your account settings.
* **Invite a user:** opens the invite page. See [Teams](../account/teams.md).

Under **Help**:

* **Video tutorials:** opens FlowFlex's YouTube channel.
* **App updates:** shows what is new in FlowFlex. A **New** badge marks unread updates.
* **Contact support:** opens the [AI Assistant](../help/ai-assistant.md). From there you can message the FlowFlex support team.

**Sign out** signs you out and returns you to the sign-in page.

### Switch branch

The **Switch branch** drawer lists the branches you can work in. Each card shows the branch's initials, name and ID. Your current branch comes first, with a **Current** tag.

1. Click your name in the top bar, then click **Switch** on the organisation card.
2. Click the branch you want, such as **Bengaluru**. You now work in that branch.

With more than six branches, use **Search branches** to find one. To add or edit branches, click **Manage**. See [Branches](../account/branches.md).

### Notifications

The bell opens **Notifications**. An orange dot means you have unread notifications. Notifications are grouped under **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This week** and **Older**. Click one to open the page or file it's about. Click **Mark all as read** to clear the dot. With no notifications, the drawer shows **No notification yet**.

You get a notification when longer tasks finish, for example:

* Audience imports and exports, and bulk actions on contacts, lists, segments and templates. A bulk action reports what happened, such as **Add tag completed — 120 contacts updated.**
* WhatsApp template status updates and template deletions.
* Broadcast metrics exports.
* Wallet recharges and credits.
* Subscription charges, cancellations and mandate reminders.
* Invoice downloads.
* Team invitations sent and accepted.
* Flow installations.

## Quick search

Press **Ctrl+S** anywhere in the app to open quick search. Type part of a page name, then pick it to jump there.

You can jump to Home, Workflow, Broadcast, Audience, Conversations, Chat Settings, Integrations, Account, Billing, Teams, Branches, Templates and Channels. Quick search leaves out pages your role cannot open, and the page you are already on.

## Organisation switcher

The bottom of the sidebar shows your organisation's name and plan, for example **Rangoli Store** and its plan. Without a plan it shows **No Active Plan**. Click it to open the switcher.

* Under **ORGANISATIONS**, click another organisation to switch to it.
* Click **+** to create a new organisation. See [Sign up and sign in](sign-up-and-sign-in.md#create-your-organisation).
* **Account Settings**, **Invite user** and **Sign Out** work like the profile drawer.

To manage your organisation's details, see [Organisation](../account/organisation.md).

## Banners

A banner at the top of the page warns you about your plan, with a button to fix the issue.

| Banner says                                                                                       | Button             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| **Your trial period ends in N days.**                                                             | **Upgrade Plan**   |
| **Your trial has expired.** or **Your plan has ended.**                                           | **Upgrade Plan**   |
| **Your subscription will end on** a date                                                          | **Upgrade Plan**   |
| **Your subscription has been cancelled.**                                                         | **Upgrade Plan**   |
| **Your account has been suspended due to a payment failure.**                                     | **Upgrade Plan**   |
| A renewal payment is pending                                                                      | **Manage Billing** |
| **Your new plan starts on** a date. **Authorize the mandate before then to keep service active.** | **Authorize now**  |

**Upgrade Plan** opens **Plans**. See [Plans and add-ons](../account/plans-and-add-ons.md) and [Billing and wallet](../account/billing-and-wallet.md).

FlowFlex may also show an announcement bar above the top bar. Click **Click here for more details.** to read more.

## AI Assistant button

The round **AI Assistant** button with a robot logo sits in the bottom-right corner of each page. On the Conversations pages, a smaller button sits near the top right instead, so it doesn't cover the chat.

Click it to ask how to do something in FlowFlex, or to check why a broadcast, flow, channel or worker isn't working. If you still need help, the assistant hands you over to the FlowFlex support team. See [AI Assistant](../help/ai-assistant.md).
