# AI Assistant

Ask the AI Assistant how to do something in FlowFlex, or let it check why a broadcast, flow, channel or worker isn't working.

The AI Assistant is a help chat inside FlowFlex, available to everyone who is signed in. It answers questions about using FlowFlex, walks you through common tasks step by step, and can look at a record you have open to find what went wrong. If it can't solve the problem, it hands you over to the FlowFlex support team.

The morning after Rangoli Store's Diwali sale, Jordan Brown, the marketing manager, sees that `Diwali sale – repeat buyers – Oct 2026` had failures. Jordan opens the broadcast and asks the AI Assistant to check it. The check finds a problem under **Delivery**, and Jordan clicks **Open failure reasons** to see which contacts didn't get the message and why.

## Open the AI Assistant

Open the panel in any of these ways:

* **AI Assistant button:** click the round button with a robot logo in the bottom-right corner of the page. On the Conversations pages, a smaller button sits near the top right.
* **Teaser:** when **Need a hand? Ask how to send a broadcast, fix a rejected template and more.** pops up next to the button, click it.
* **Contact support:** click your name in the top bar, then **Contact support** under **Help**.
* **Home page:** on the **Need a hand?** card, click **Ask the AI Assistant**.

The **AI Assistant** panel opens over the page you're on. Click **Close** to hide it.

## Ask a question

1. Open the AI Assistant. It shows **How can I help today?**
2. Pick a question under **Popular questions**, such as **Why was my template rejected?** or **What is the 24-hour reply window?**, or type your own question in the message box.
3. Read the answer.
4. To go further, type in **Ask a follow-up**. Press **Enter** to send, or **Shift + Enter** for a new line.

Other popular questions include **Why did my last broadcast fail?**, **Why isn't my flow working?**, **Why isn't my Worker replying?** and **Is my WhatsApp number healthy?**

To learn a task from start to finish, pick one under **Step-by-step guides**. The guide lists the steps, and they tick off as you complete them.

To start over on a different topic, click **New chat**.

## Check a broadcast, flow, channel or worker

When you open a broadcast, flow, channel or worker and then open the AI Assistant, it offers to check that record. The offer names the record and says what it will look at:

* **Check this broadcast:** its setup, sending and delivery.
* **Check this flow:** how it starts, its steps and the sending number.
* **Check this channel:** its connection, quality and messaging limit.
* **Check this Worker:** its trigger, number, rules and recent runs.

1. Open the record, such as the broadcast `Diwali sale – repeat buyers – Oct 2026`.
2. Open the AI Assistant and click the check it offers, such as **Check this broadcast**.
3. Wait a few seconds while it checks. The result shows how many things it looked at, such as **Checked 9 things**.
4. Read the results. They're grouped by area; for a broadcast, under **Setup**, **Sending** and **Delivery**. Each group says **No problems found** or how many problems it found, such as **1 problem found**.
5. Click a link next to a problem to go straight to the detail, such as **Open failure reasons** or **Open failed contacts**.

For Jordan's broadcast, **Setup** and **Sending** show **No problems found**, and **Delivery** shows **1 problem found**. **Open failure reasons** lists the failed messages by reason. See [Results and errors](../broadcasts/results-and-errors.md) for what each reason means.

> [!NOTE]
> Answers can be wrong, and the assistant never changes your account. It only reads your setup and explains what it finds. You make any changes yourself.

## Rate an answer

After a check, the assistant asks **Did this solve it?**

* Click **Yes** if it fixed your problem. The answer is marked **Solved**.
* Click **No** if it didn't. The assistant offers to hand you over to the support team.

After other answers, you see **Was this helpful?** Click the thumbs-up (**Helpful**) or thumbs-down (**Not helpful**) icon.

## Contact the support team

When you're still stuck, the assistant shows **Still stuck? Our team can look at your account and take it from here.**

1. Click **Contact support**.
2. On **Message our support team**, check the text under **Your message**. It sums up your chat, and you can change it. Your organisation and the page you're on are added so the team can help faster.
3. Click **Open WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the message filled in. Nothing is sent until you tap send in WhatsApp.

## Go back to an earlier chat

1. Click **Recent chats** at the top of the panel.
2. Click a chat to open it again. The chat you're in is marked **Current**.

Chats are kept for 30 days. Click **Back to chat** to return without opening another one.
